# Banksia Creek Investigation

## Case File 26-417: The Missing Community Fund

An interactive Year 7/8 Humanities investigation developed for Civics and Citizenship.

Students take on the role of a legal investigation team examining the disappearance of $18,750 from the fictional Banksia Creek Community Association in Victoria.

Throughout the investigation, students will:

- analyse evidence
- construct a timeline of events
- examine witness statements
- distinguish between facts, claims and inferences
- identify contradictions in evidence
- apply Australian and Victorian legal principles
- examine the rule of law and principles of justice
- develop a case theory
- prepare a lawyer's opening statement
- communicate their final case through a presentation or investigative news article

## Curriculum Focus

Victorian Curriculum 2.0 – Civics and Citizenship, Years 7–8

The investigation focuses on:

- democratic institutions and values
- the Australian legal system
- the rule of law
- courts and the court hierarchy
- statutory and common law
- citizen participation in lawmaking
- evaluating evidence and competing perspectives
- communicating evidence-based conclusions
