Incident Summary

At approximately 8:12 am on Monday morning, Community Association Treasurer Claire Bennett reported that money was missing from the Banksia Creek Youth Recreation Project account.

$18,750 missing

The account contained money raised by local residents for the construction of a new youth recreation area.

Initial account information suggests that the money was transferred electronically on Friday evening.

Several people were present at the Banksia Creek Community Centre during the afternoon and evening.

Known Information

  • The recreation project account contained the fundraising money before Friday evening.
  • An electronic transfer appears to have removed $18,750 from the account.
  • Several people were inside the community centre on Friday.
  • The office computer could be used to access the association's online banking.
  • More than one authorised adult knew how to access the project account.
  • A youth committee meeting was held at the centre that afternoon.
  • A local councillor visited the community centre.
  • No forced entry or physical damage was reported.

Not Yet Established

  • Who completed the transfer.
  • Why the transfer was made.
  • Where the money went.
  • Whether the transfer was deliberately dishonest.
  • Whether any person committed a criminal offence.

People Connected to the Case

Claire Bennett Community Association Treasurer. Reported the missing money.
Alex Morgan 16-year-old youth committee representative who attended Friday's meeting.
Daniel Reed Local councillor who had publicly criticised the recreation project.
Sam Patel Cleaner working at the community centre on Friday evening.
Mia Tran Youth committee member who attended the meeting with Alex.
