Summary

At approximately 8:12 am on Monday, Community Association Treasurer Claire Bennett reported that $18,750 was missing from the Banksia Creek Youth Recreation Project account.

The account had contained enough money to begin construction of a new community recreation area. The missing amount appears to have been transferred electronically on Friday evening.

Known information

  • The project account was active and contained the fundraising money before the transfer.
  • Several people were at the community centre on Friday afternoon and evening.
  • The office computer could be used to access the association's online banking.
  • More than one authorised adult knew how to access the project account.
  • A youth committee meeting was held at the centre on Friday.
  • A local councillor also visited the centre that evening.
  • No physical damage or forced entry was reported.

Not yet established

  • Who completed the transfer.
  • Why the transfer was made.
  • Whether the money was intentionally stolen.
  • Whether any person committed a criminal offence.
