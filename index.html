Investigator Protocol

  1. Record before judging. Build your timeline from evidence.
  2. Cite exhibits. Refer to evidence by exhibit letter or title.
  3. Cross-check. Compare witnesses with timestamps and documents.
  4. Look both ways. Record evidence that supports and weakens your theory.
  5. Apply the law. Suspicious behaviour is not automatically a criminal offence.
  6. Revise. Strong investigators change their theory when stronger evidence appears.

Evidence Language

FACT Directly supported by reliable evidence.

CLAIM Something a person says happened.

INFERENCE A conclusion drawn from evidence.

UNKNOWN There is not enough evidence yet.

CONTRADICTION Two important accounts or pieces of evidence conflict.
