How to investigate

  1. Record facts before conclusions.
  2. Use exhibit numbers.
  3. Check times carefully.
  4. Compare witnesses with documents.
  5. Separate suspicion from proof.
  6. Change your theory if new evidence is stronger.

Evidence language

FACT — directly supported by reliable evidence.

CLAIM — something a person says happened.

INFERENCE — a conclusion drawn from evidence.

UNKNOWN — not enough evidence yet.

CONTRADICTION — two pieces of evidence cannot both be accurate as stated.
