INVESTIGATOR INSTRUCTION: A witness statement is evidence, but it is not automatically a fact. People can remember times incorrectly, make assumptions, misunderstand what they saw, or leave out details. Cross-check every important claim.
Statement Set 01

Witness Accounts

Claire Bennett Community Association Treasurer
Statement 1
“I arrived before the youth meeting because I had some association paperwork to finish. I remember receiving an email about a security problem with the project account. It looked serious and said action had to be taken before six o’clock.

I joined the youth meeting at the beginning, then left at around quarter past five to deal with paperwork in the office. I was worried about the account and I followed what I believed were the bank’s security instructions. I thought I was moving the project money somewhere safe while the bank checked the problem.

I did not realise anything was wrong until Monday morning when I checked the account again and the money had not returned. I immediately reported it.”
Approx. interview timeMonday 9:05 am
Key issueAccount access / suspicious email
Cross-check with: Exhibits C, D and E.
Investigator prompts
Connect it: Which parts of Claire’s statement are supported by the meeting record and email?
Judge it: Does Claire admitting she made a transfer automatically prove theft? What would you still need to know?
Alex Morgan Youth Committee Representative, age 16
Statement 2
“I got to the centre a little after five for the youth committee meeting. We were talking about the recreation area and how council delays were making everything harder.

I stayed in the meeting room for nearly the whole meeting. I went into the hallway once to get water, but I did not go into the office and I did not use the computer. I remember Councillor Reed coming in while the meeting was still going. He looked annoyed and spoke to Claire near the hall.

I left when the meeting finished — about 5:45, I think. Mum was picking me up near the oval. I only heard about the missing money later.”
Approx. interview timeMonday 10:20 am
Key issueLocation / access / timing
Cross-check with: Exhibits B, D and F.
Investigator prompts
Check it: Alex says “about 5:45.” CCTV shows 5:48. Is that a serious contradiction? Explain.
Judge it: Which parts of Alex’s account are facts, claims or inferences?
Daniel Reed Local Councillor
Statement 3
“I went to the community centre to collect copies of project documents. I have questioned the cost of the recreation project, but that is part of my job as a councillor. Disagreeing with a project is not the same as wanting the money to disappear.

I arrived sometime after five-twenty. Claire was busy and I waited near the hall. I spoke with her briefly about the project and then collected the documents I needed.

I did not enter the office while I was there. I remember seeing Alex near the hallway at one point, but I cannot say exactly what time it was or where Alex was going. I left before six.”
Approx. interview timeMonday 11:15 am
Key issuePossible motive / location
Cross-check with: Exhibits B and D.
Investigator prompts
Question it: Is Councillor Reed’s opposition to the project evidence of a crime, or only possible motive?
Reliability: How useful is the statement “I saw Alex near the hallway” when the witness cannot give a time or destination?
Sam Patel Community Centre Cleaner
Statement 4
“I started cleaning the storage area and toilets just after five. There were people coming and going because of the youth meeting.

At some point after 5:25, I walked past the office doorway and saw someone sitting at the computer. I only saw the person from behind for a moment. I thought it was an adult, but I could not clearly see the face.

Later, I saw Claire walk quickly through the hall. She looked worried and was holding her phone. I did not hear what she said. I finished my work and locked the main doors at about 6:18.”
Approx. interview timeMonday 12:05 pm
Key issueOffice computer / unidentified person
Cross-check with: Exhibits A, B and E.
Investigator prompts
Important: What did Sam actually observe? What did Sam assume?
Evaluate: How much weight should you give this statement when identifying who used the computer?
Mia Tran Youth Committee Member
Statement 5
“The youth committee meeting started at about five. Alex was there and was talking a lot about the recreation project. Alex was frustrated about the delays, but definitely wanted the project to go ahead.

Claire left the meeting after about fifteen minutes. Later, near the end of the meeting, I went into the hall. I saw Claire coming from the office area looking stressed. I think she said something like, ‘I just need to sort this bank thing out,’ but I cannot remember the exact words.

I also remember Councillor Reed being annoyed about the project earlier. Alex left just after the meeting. I sent Alex a message because I realised they had already gone.”
Approx. interview timeMonday 1:30 pm
Key issueClaire’s behaviour / Alex’s timing / project dispute
Cross-check with: Exhibits B, D, E and F.
Investigator prompts
Cross-check: Which parts of Mia’s statement are supported by CCTV and the text messages?
Reliability: Mia says Claire “said something like…” Why should an investigator treat the exact wording cautiously?
